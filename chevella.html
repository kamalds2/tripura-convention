Questions About Booking in Chevella

What is the guest capacity at Tripura Convention Chevella?

Our air-conditioned main banquet hall comfortably hosts 500 to 1,000 guests, while our open celebration lawn can comfortably host up to 2,000+ guests for grand weddings, receptions, and public functions.

Is catering and event decoration available in-house?

Yes! We offer full-service catering with customizable South Indian, North Indian, and Continental menus, alongside royal stage décor, floral styling, sound systems, and drone photography packages.

How can I book a date or check pricing?

You can call our booking team directly at +91 9696396666, send a WhatsApp message, or click "Book Now" to submit your event details for an instant quote.
